THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Configuration changes can make earlier failover evidence stale.

FactoryDR tested whether structured, deterministic logic can identify when evidence supporting a designated backup production route is still current, needs revalidation, is missing or blocked, or cannot be resolved from available facts.

COMMERCIAL FALSIFICATION

A successful mechanism was not enough to justify a startup.

Evidence for the problem

Change/requalification work is real and recurring; relevant evidence is often digital; organizational owners exist.

Evidence against the company

PLM, ERP, QMS and SCRM platforms already cover substantial portions; a separate buyer/budget for the residual layer was not established.
