# Claim boundaries

FactoryDR is an **independent research project**, not a deployed manufacturing product.

## Supported technical claim

A frozen deterministic mechanism correctly tracked applicability of **synthetic** backup-manufacturing evidence under the defined synthetic configuration changes in `FDR-P0-1.0`.

## What the project did not establish

It did **not** establish:

- real backup-factory capability or readiness;
- manufacturing-transfer success;
- production quality or safety;
- regulatory/customer compliance;
- customer demand or adoption;
- willingness to pay;
- production savings or downtime reduction;
- standalone SaaS viability.

## Commercial disposition

`PARK — SERVICES / INTERNAL TOOL / RESEARCH`

The workflow appears real and recurring, but public evidence did not establish that the residual cross-system failover-evidence gap is sufficiently under-served or separately budgeted to justify a standalone SaaS product.

## Portfolio interpretation

The value of this project is the research process: hypothesis framing, deterministic policy design, frozen evaluation, held-out testing, primary-source evidence review, incumbent substitution analysis, and a disciplined decision not to build when the commercial evidence did not clear the threshold.
