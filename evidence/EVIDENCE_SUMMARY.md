# Commercial evidence summary

The commercial stage used public-evidence shadow validation because direct customer outreach was not available. This is **not** equivalent to customer discovery.

## Evidence grading

- **A** — concrete real-company workflow, contract, requirement, internal program or implementation evidence
- **B** — detailed practitioner/process evidence strongly showing recurring operational work
- **C** — vendor/product documentation useful for thesis or incumbent capability analysis
- **D** — generic commentary or unsupported opinion

The final dossier records **18 evidence records: 10 A-grade, 1 B-grade and 7 C-grade**.

## What survived

- Manufacturing/site/process/tooling/source/test changes can trigger review or requalification.
- Alternate-production readiness is maintained as an ongoing operational concern in multiple organizations.
- Much of the relevant configuration/evidence state is digital or digitizable.
- Organizational ownership exists across supplier quality, manufacturing engineering, operations, supply chain and continuity functions.

## What weakened the standalone-company thesis

- Modern PLM systems can model plant-specific BOMs, process plans, resources, configuration changes and manufacturing impact.
- ERP/manufacturing systems can propagate or flag structure/work-definition changes.
- QMS tools already own supplier qualification, change, PPAP, audit and corrective-action workflows.
- SCRM platforms already track meaningful alternate-site qualification/readiness data.
- Sophisticated manufacturers may build internal continuity/readiness capabilities.
- A separate economic buyer/budget for the exact FactoryDR layer was not established.
